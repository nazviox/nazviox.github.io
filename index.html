About Me

Academic background, qualifications, and research domain focus

I have been serving as an Associate Professor of Geography at Cooch Behar Panchanan Barma University since 2021. Prior to this, I served as Assistant Professor at Sitalkuchi College and held various guest faculty positions teaching postgraduate courses.

My academic journey began at Asutosh College (University of Calcutta) with a B.Sc. (Honours) in Geography with Geology, followed by an M.Sc. from the University of Calcutta. I was awarded the National Scholarship for academic excellence. I completed my Ph.D. at the University of North Bengal in 2018.

I have undertaken advanced technical training in Remote Sensing and GIS from IIRS, Dehradun and DST, West Bengal. My primary focus includes spatial multi-criteria decision making (MCDM), urban environmental modeling, water quality assessment, and social geography.

Upcoming Research & Ongoing Projects

Active investigations in Sub-Himalayan geography, hydro-social dynamics, and environmental management

Ongoing Project (2025–2027)

Sub-Himalayan River Basin Hydro-Dynamics & Eco-Tourism

Evaluating riverbank erosion, groundwater depletion using GLDAS datasets, and geotourism site suitability across the Eastern Dooars region using machine learning and coupled E-PROMETHEE-II MCDM models.

Book Project (In Preparation)

Geospatial Intelligence for Urban Waste & Sanitation (SDG-6 & 11)

A comprehensive monograph focusing on Machine Learning & ANN-based prediction models for municipal solid waste management, drinking water quality index (WQI), and WASH service equity in Eastern India.
